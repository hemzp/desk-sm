A small-monitor(sm) to be placed on my desk to display a vinyl playing with my current songs' album art which is rotating as it plays and stationery when paused. Connected via Spotify API.

Other functionality include a weather display setting and the ability to see your calendar for the day. There are two push buttons which can be used to toggle the song and play/pause, there is also a rotary encoder to select between songs, weather, calendar.

- I listen to a lot of music and love album art so I thought it'd be aesthetic
- Weather + Calendar is crucial info. for me specially when I am in college, need to track my Class times, office hours, extrac. activities, meetings and more and since its Seattle I have to know what the weather is (raining or not raining)
